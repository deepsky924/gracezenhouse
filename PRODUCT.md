# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: local women aged roughly 30–55 in Rancho Cucamonga and the surrounding Inland Empire. They book regular facials and anti-aging care as ongoing self-care, and many come back about monthly. They land on the site to choose a treatment, check its price, and book it.

Also served: Reiki clients (in person or at a distance), and waxing clients of all genders.

## Product Purpose

A multi-page marketing and booking site for Grace Zen House, a holistic skincare and energy-healing studio. It helps visitors find the right treatment and book it directly in Square. It replaces an earlier single-page React site. Success means booked appointments and strong rankings in local search for each service.

## Positioning

- **One practitioner, both disciplines:** an esthetician who is also a Reiki master, treating skin and nervous system together.
- **Personal, one-on-one care:** a small private studio where clients see Grace every visit, never a rotating staff.
- **Professional product lines:** Circadia and Hale & Hush, across 14 specialized facials.
- **Inclusive and welcoming:** all genders and ages. Waxing and facials are explicitly for men too.

## Operating Context

- Booking happens only through Square. Every service has its own booking link, and there's a general link.
- Visits are **by appointment only**. There are no public opening hours.
- Studio: 7365 Carnelian St, Suite 222A, Rancho Cucamonga, CA 91730, in the Haven Village Shopping Center.
- Contact: (626) 806-5016 · salientbeauty@icloud.com · Instagram @ggbeauty.ie. That's the only Instagram account to use; don't use @salientbeauty.ie.
- Distance Reiki is done remotely, so it can serve clients anywhere.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site generated by `build.mjs` from `src/data.mjs`, and served by nginx in Docker on Coolify. All business facts live in `src/data.mjs`.
- URLs from the live site are kept for SEO: `/services/facial-treatments/<slug>/`, `/services/reiki-services/<slug>/`, `/services/waxing-services/<slug>/`. Every page needs a unique title and description, a canonical link, and OG/Twitter tags, following the neverhunt.ai pattern.
- Menu: 14 facials ($110–$200), Reiki $111, 30-minute Distance Reiki $88, and about 25 waxing areas ($10–$300) plus two add-ons. Prices come from the live site.
- There's no contact form or backend. Visitors book through Square or by phone or email.
- No API keys or secrets in client-side code, ever. The old site leaked one.
- **Undecided:** the owner's full name and credentials (placeholders for now); fuller owner-written descriptions for eight thin facial pages; analytics provider (the site is ready for Plausible); whether @ggbeauty.ie and the salientbeauty email reflect a rebrand.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Grace Zen House. Tagline: "Holistic Skincare & Energy Healing."
- The client wants sage green kept in the palette, because it signals calm, peace and zen.
- Feel: calm, warm, premium, never templated or generic.
- Health-adjacent claims stay cosmetic and modest: "helps clear breakouts", not "acne reduction". Stronger claims need the owner's approval.

## Evidence on Hand

- Five real Google reviews, with names, in `src/data.mjs`. The rating is 5.0 across 24 Google reviews. Don't invent reviews. The live site's "Sarah L." review was dropped as unverifiable.
- Service descriptions, prep and aftercare notes come from the live site.
- **Real studio photos are coming** from the owner: the space, treatments and Grace herself. **Photos from @ggbeauty.ie may be used.** Until then the image slots are labelled placeholders. Don't use stock photos presented as the studio.
- No logo yet. The site uses a placeholder ensō mark.

## Product Principles

1. **Booking is always one step away.** Every service, on every page, links straight to its Square booking.
2. **Personal over corporate.** Speak as one practitioner in one studio, not a spa chain.
3. **Truth over polish.** Use only real reviews, real prices and real photos. Mark placeholders clearly instead of faking them.
4. **Local and findable.** Every service earns its own well-structured page for Rancho Cucamonga searches.
5. **Calm is the product.** The site should feel as restorative as the visit, without losing clarity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Aim for WCAG 2.1 AA: readable contrast, 44px tap targets on mobile, full keyboard access, and support for reduced motion.
- Copy uses inclusive language for all genders and ages.
